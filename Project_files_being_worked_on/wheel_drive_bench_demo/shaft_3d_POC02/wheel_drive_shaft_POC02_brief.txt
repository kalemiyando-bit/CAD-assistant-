SYSTEM INTENT

A small, bench-only, direct-drive wheel module: a wheel and hub on a wheel-side
shaft, supported by a bearing/support, coupled to a small brushed DC motor. The
user describes a motor-side shaft connected through the coupling; whether this
is a separate part depends on the motor selected. No external gear reduction and
no reverse are intended. The wheel speed should be measurable.

The requested controls are GAS and BRAKE buttons. A tap changes the speed target
by one level. Holding GAS raises speed gradually; holding BRAKE lowers it to
zero. Include a separate handbrake/emergency-stop control.

For this simple proof of concept, the current contract proposes a 300 rpm
commanded speed cap, 25 rpm per tap, and a 30 rpm/s hold ramp. These are
preliminary assumptions, not validated controller settings or safe operating
limits. At the existing 80 mm wheel envelope, 300 rpm corresponds to about
1.26 m/s rim speed (calculated, not measured).

FIRST 3D PART

- Part: wheel-side shaft.
- Geometry: one straight cylinder, Ø6 mm × 46 mm.
- Units: millimetres.
- Coordinate frame: right-handed Z-up; shaft axis along global +Y; cylinder base
  center at the origin.
- These dimensions reuse the A0 workflow envelope because the user authorized a
  simple preliminary choice. They are not approved fits or interfaces.
- The Fusion model should contain an editable native sketch and extrusion. Its
  sketch is an internal feature of the 3D model, not a separate 2D deliverable.
- The model has no shoulders, keyway, threads, retention feature, selected
  material, bearing fit, or verified motor/coupling interface.

SAFETY AND VALIDATION

The project notes list a 7,400 rpm no-load speed for an unselected motor
candidate. With an 80 mm wheel, that gives a calculated rim speed near 31 m/s;
this is not measured operation and does not prove the candidate is suitable.
Do not recommend powered testing until a physical guard, current-limited test
procedure, speed limit, and actual stop behavior are defined for selected
hardware. The proposed 300 rpm cap is not a safety certification.

Replit cannot run the user's desktop Fusion application. This package supplies
the one-part model specification and Fusion script needed to create a native
.f3d in Fusion. Fusion execution, archive reopening, dimension inspection, fit,
strength, and manufacturing readiness remain unverified until checked with the
actual application and components.

The earlier POC-01 DXF is retained as a prior handoff. It is not part of this
3D-only deliverable.
