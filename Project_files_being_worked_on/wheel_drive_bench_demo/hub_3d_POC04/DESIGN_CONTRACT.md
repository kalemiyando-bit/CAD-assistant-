## Interfaces and constraints

- **Units:** Millimetres.
- **Coordinate frame / origin:** Right-handed, Z-up part frame. Hub axis is +Y. Center of the base annular face is at the part origin; the part extends from Y=0 to Y=8 mm.
- **Envelope and mounting interfaces:** 30 mm outside diameter, 6 mm nominal bore, 8 mm width. The bore is a placeholder only; it does not define a fit, tolerance, retention, or connection to the shaft or wheel rim.
- **Loads, motion, electrical limits, and safety conditions:** Future wheel rotation about the shaft axis. No loads or operating limits are defined by this part. No powered testing is approved.
- **Materials and manufacturing constraints:** Unspecified.
- **Known purchased components / source:** None selected for this part.
