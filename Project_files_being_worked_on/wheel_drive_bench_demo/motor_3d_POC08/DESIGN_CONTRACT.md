# Wheel Drive Bench Demonstrator — Motor Envelope Contract

**Revision:** POC-08 / preliminary 3D-only candidate envelope  
**Status:** Model specification prepared. Mabuchi FF-130SH-14230 remains an unselected candidate; this envelope is not an approved motor selection or operating rating.

## Purpose and operating context

- **Design name:** FF-130 Motor Candidate Envelope
- **Intended function:** Reserve the approximate external body envelope of the motor candidate listed in the parent contract.
- **Users and environment:** Bench-only proof of concept.
- **Target software and version:** Autodesk Fusion on Windows; exact version unknown.
- **Agent-accessible tools and limits:** Existing Fusion JSON generator supports box envelopes. Fusion itself is run by the user.

## Interfaces and constraints

- **Units:** Millimetres.
- **Coordinate frame / origin:** Right-handed, Z-up. Body length is along +Y. The local origin is the lower X/Y/Z corner before axis rotation.
- **Envelope and mounting interfaces:** 20.4 mm along X, 15.4 mm across the motor body section, 25.1 mm body length along +Y. No mounting ears, screw holes, output shaft, or electrical terminals are modeled.
- **Loads, motion, electrical limits, and safety conditions:** This body envelope does not establish motor suitability, loaded speed, torque, current, or safe operation.
- **Materials and manufacturing constraints:** Simplified purchased-part envelope; no manufacturing process is specified.
- **Known purchased components / source:** Mabuchi FF-130SH-14230, candidate only; details and source links are recorded in the parent design contract.

## Research scope

**User sources only.** This envelope reuses the candidate data already recorded in the project. No additional external research was performed.
