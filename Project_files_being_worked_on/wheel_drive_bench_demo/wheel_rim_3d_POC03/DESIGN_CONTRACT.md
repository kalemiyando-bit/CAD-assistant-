## Interfaces and constraints

- **Units:** Millimetres.
- **Coordinate frame / origin:** Right-handed, Z-up part frame. Axis is global +Y. The center of the base annular face is at the part origin; the solid extends from Y=0 to Y=24 mm.
- **Envelope and mounting interfaces:** 80 mm outside diameter, 6 mm nominal bore, 24 mm width. The bore is only a placeholder; no fit, tolerance, retention, or shaft engagement is defined.
- **Loads, motion, electrical limits, and safety conditions:** Wheel rotates about the shaft axis in the eventual assembly. Loads and operating speed are not defined by this part model. The model is not approved for powered testing.
- **Materials and manufacturing constraints:** Unspecified.
- **Known purchased components / source:** None selected for this part.
