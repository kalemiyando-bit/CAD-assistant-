# Wheel Drive Bench Demonstrator — Bearing Support Contract

**Revision:** Preliminary 3D-only part  
**Status:** Model specification prepared; dimensions and interfaces are assumptions. Not approved for manufacture, assembly, or powered testing.

## Purpose and operating context

- **Design name:** Bearing Support Plate Placeholder
- **Intended function:** A simple plate envelope with a centered opening for the preliminary bearing envelope.
- **Users and environment:** Bench-only proof of concept; not a vehicle or payload support.
- **Target software and version:** Autodesk Fusion on Windows; exact version unknown.
- **Agent-accessible tools and limits:** The existing Fusion generator supports a plate with a centered bore. Fusion itself is run by the user.

## Interfaces and constraints

- **Units:** Millimetres.
- **Coordinate frame / origin:** Right-handed, Z-up. Plate thickness is along +Y. The local origin is the lower X/Z corner of the base face at Y=0.
- **Envelope and mounting interfaces:** 30 mm along X, 30 mm along Z, 4 mm thick along Y, centered 12 mm bore. The bore is only nominal and does not define a bearing fit or retention.
- **Loads, motion, electrical limits, and safety conditions:** No loads, mounting fasteners, restraint, or operating limits are defined.
- **Materials and manufacturing constraints:** Unspecified.
- **Known purchased components / source:** The 12 mm bearing is an unselected envelope placeholder.
