## Interfaces and constraints

- **Units:** Millimetres.
- **Coordinate frame / origin:** Right-handed, Z-up part frame. The bearing axis is +Y. The center of the base annular face is at the part origin; the envelope extends from Y=0 to Y=4 mm.
- **Envelope and mounting interfaces:** 12 mm outside diameter, 6 mm inside diameter, 4 mm width. The outer diameter and bore are placeholders, not tolerance or fit specifications.
- **Loads, motion, electrical limits, and safety conditions:** The inner ring represents a shaft-support envelope only. Bearing type, speed rating, load rating, lubrication, seals, and operating conditions are unspecified.
- **Materials and manufacturing constraints:** Unspecified; the model represents a purchased-part envelope rather than a custom bearing design.
- **Known purchased components / source:** No bearing manufacturer, series, or part number selected.

## Assumptions, risks, and open questions

| ID | Item | Impact if wrong | How to resolve | Owner/status |

| A-01 | 12 × 6 × 4 mm values are preliminary envelope assumptions. | The selected bearing may not fit its support or shaft. | Select or measure the bearing and update the envelope before assembly. | Preliminary assumption |
| A-02 | A plain annular cylinder is sufficient to represent the bearing's external envelope. | It does not represent rolling elements, races, cage, seals, or internal construction. | Use supplier CAD or a more detailed model if those features affect packaging. | Preliminary assumption |
