## Required return

1. Files created or changed, within owned paths only.
2. Decisions and changes, with requirement IDs.
3. Checks run, exact results, failures, and unavailable checks.
4. Sourced facts and links, if research was allowed.
5. Assumptions, unresolved risks, and proposed change requests.
6. Confirmation that no other agent's files were overwritten or deleted.

Do not mark the whole design complete. The coordinator integrates and the reviewer checks it.
